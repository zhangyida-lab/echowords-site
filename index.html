遇见的词。

前往 Chrome 网上应用店了解数据如何保存

采集

看到好词，顺手记下。

网页文章

选中词语后按下已配置的快捷键，保存词语、附近文本及来源；单词采集不会打断当前选择。

YouTube 字幕

从当前屏幕上可见的字幕中挑选词语，并记住视频位置；词语选择结束后恢复原来的播放状态。

PDF 与复习

在 Chrome 的 PDF 阅读器中通过选中文本的右键菜单保存；在单词本中查看来源、翻译和复习记录。

下一次更新 · 开发中

看见音标，也能听见单词。

下一版将提供发音辅助和按网站开启的悬浮按钮，让采词与回顾更方便。

01

采集后补齐音标

保存英文单词后查询音标，优先显示美式 IPA。查询失败不会影响保存，你也可以在词表中手动重试。

02

点击朗读单词

在 Chrome 词表和复习页点击朗读，优先使用设备上的美式英语语音；在复习页播放时不会翻卡。

03

按网站开启采集按钮

逐个授权网站后显示悬浮按钮，收集多个英文词或短语后统一保存。按钮可全局关闭或逐站隐藏；未授权网站仍可使用快捷键。

此更新尚未发布到 Chrome 网上应用店，当前商店版本可能暂不包含这些功能。了解音标查询如何处理数据

Next update · In development

See the pronunciation. Hear the word.

The next update adds pronunciation tools and optional per-website capture buttons for collecting and revisiting words.

01

IPA after capture

English words get an IPA lookup after they are saved, with US pronunciation preferred. A failed lookup never affects the saved word, and you can retry from the notebook.

02

Listen to a word

Play a word from the Chrome notebook or review page using a local English voice when available. Listening during review does not flip the card.

03

Enable buttons per website

Authorize individual websites to collect English words and phrases with a floating button. Hide buttons globally or per website; the shortcut remains available without website opt-in.

You choose whether to sync

Your notebook starts locally.

After you enable sync and authorize Google Drive, EchoWords exchanges Chrome-captured vocabulary through its hidden app data folder. iPhone ratings for those words also sync, so you can continue reviewing on another iPhone. Wordbooks imported on iPhone stay on that device.

The first sync requires Google authorization. You can pause automatic sync in Chrome or on iPhone. No EchoWords account is needed.
