0.1.2

已发布

发布日期：2026 年 11 月 4 日

新增功能

  • 保存英文单词后查询 IPA 音标，优先显示美式发音；查询失败不影响保存，可在词表中重试。
  • 在词表和复习页点击朗读单词，优先使用设备上的美式英语语音。
  • 文章多选采集：挑选多个英文单词或短语，预览语境后统一保存。
  • 按网站授权悬浮采集按钮，支持全局关闭、逐站隐藏，以及在设置中恢复显示。

体验优化

  • 词表增加分页，每页可选 10、20、50 或 100 个单词，并记住你的选择；搜索和筛选覆盖完整词库。
  • 桌面词库采用双栏阅读布局：搜索和筛选保持可见，词表独立滚动，顶部工具栏和底部分页随时可用。
  • 低频设置分组折叠，悬浮采集栏采用与 App 呼应的书页图标，隐藏品牌文字，保留三点菜单。
  • 词库侧栏新增应用主页和隐私政策链接，支持中英文界面。

问题修复

  • 隐藏的网站保留恢复入口；曾被旧版隐藏的网站，可先打开该网页，再点击扩展工具栏图标进入设置恢复。
  • 调整左右栏滚动范围，避免滚动左侧设置时带动右侧词表。
  • 采集预览更新时保留展开状态、滚动位置和键盘焦点。

音标查询仅向 Free Dictionary API 发送单词。了解数据如何处理。

0.1.2

Released

Released November 4, 2026

New features

  • Look up IPA after saving English words, preferring US pronunciation. Failed lookups do not affect saving and can be retried from the notebook.
  • Listen to words from the notebook and review page, preferring an on-device US English voice.
  • Collect multiple English words or phrases from articles, preview their context, and save them together.
  • Enable floating capture buttons per website, turn them off globally, hide them on individual sites, and restore them in settings.

Improvements

  • Paginated vocabulary with 10, 20, 50 or 100 words per page and a saved page-size preference. Search and filters cover the full notebook.
  • A two-column desktop workspace keeps search and filters visible while the word list scrolls independently between its toolbar and page controls.
  • Collapsible settings and a compact floating control with a book icon inspired by the app, no brand text, and a separate three-dot action.
  • Homepage and privacy-policy links in the notebook sidebar, with English and Chinese labels.

Fixes

  • Hidden websites keep a restore action. For sites hidden by older versions, open the site and click the extension toolbar icon to restore the button in settings.
  • Separate scroll areas prevent scrolling the left settings panel from moving the vocabulary list.
  • Capture preview updates preserve expanded context, scroll position and keyboard focus.

IPA lookup sends only the word to Free Dictionary API. Read how data is handled.
