iOS 应用隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 27 日

语栖 iPhone 应用用于查看和复习你在 Chrome 扩展中保存的词语，也支持导入你自己的词书。应用不会在 iPhone 上采集网页文字或读取剪贴板。词库和复习更改先保存在设备上，离线时仍可使用。

设备上保存什么

从 Chrome 同步来的词条可能包含音标及其 Wiktionary 来源链接。iPhone 应用只在本地显示这些信息，不向词典发起查询。

应用在本地保存已同步的词语、翻译、来源标题与网址、原文语境、采集时间、视频位置、复习计划及评分记录。你导入的词书文件、词书复习进度、练习筛选条件、每日推荐和设置也保存在这台 iPhone 上。应用不附带第三方词书；只有你从“文件”中选择导入时，才会读取对应文件。

Apple 翻译

在 iOS 18 或更新版本中，当你查看尚无译文的 Chrome 词条时，应用可使用 Apple 的设备端翻译功能处理该词及最近一次保存的原文语境。iOS 可能先请求下载语言包。译文先保存在本机；启用 Google Drive 同步后，词语译文和语境译文会随词库快照上传至你的 Drive 应用数据目录。导入的词书不会自动翻译。iOS 17 仍显示待翻译状态。

Google Drive 同步

连接 Google 账号后，应用仅请求访问该应用在 Google Drive 中的隐藏应用数据目录 appDataFolder。它会下载、合并和上传 Chrome 采集词库的设备快照，内容包括词语、翻译、来源网址及语境、复习计划和删除标记。iPhone 对 Chrome 词语产生的评分事件也会以单独的学习历史文件同步；其中包含规范化后的词语、复习时间、新词标记和评分，用于在你的其他 iPhone 上恢复每日计数与薄弱词练习。

自动同步默认开启，在启动、返回前台及评分后尝试运行；你可以在设置中关闭，仍可手动点“立即同步”。应用关闭期间，iOS 不保证后台同步。导入的词书及其复习进度、设置、练习筛选条件和每日推荐不会上传到 Drive。同步失败时，本地词库与评分会保留，等待下次重试。

应用通过 Google Drive API 读取授权账号的标识和邮箱地址，以显示同步账号并避免将既有词库上传到另一个账号。Google Sign-In 负责登录。Drive 文件按设备保存于你的 Google 账号，不会出现在普通“我的云端硬盘”文件列表中。语栖没有开发者后台，不会将词库发送给开发者服务器、广告或分析服务。

来源链接与提醒

你主动打开词语来源时，浏览器会访问对应网站，该网站的隐私政策适用。每日学习提醒由 iOS 在本地安排；只有你开启提醒时才请求通知权限。通知内容不包含词语、语境或来源网址。

保留、删除与退出登录

你可以在应用中删除从 Chrome 同步的单词。删除标记先保存在本机，并在之后的同步中传播到其他设备。退出 Google 登录会停止访问 Drive，但保留本机数据和原账号绑定；移除 iPhone 应用会删除该设备的本地数据，但不会自动删除 Drive 中的文件。云端应用数据由你的 Google 账号管理，你可以通过 Google Drive 的应用管理功能移除。

如对隐私或数据删除有疑问，请联系 acboo2020@gmail.com。数据使用方式变化时，我们会更新本政策。

EchoWords · iPhone app

iOS App Privacy Policy

Updated October 27, 2026

The EchoWords iPhone app displays and reviews words saved with the Chrome extension and lets you import your own wordbooks. It does not capture webpage text or read the clipboard on iPhone. Vocabulary and review changes are saved on the device first, so you can continue studying offline.

Data stored on your device

Words synced from Chrome may include IPA and a Wiktionary source link. The iPhone app displays these locally and does not contact the dictionary.

The app stores synced words, translations, source titles and URLs, original context, capture times, video positions, review schedules, and ratings locally. Imported wordbook files, their review progress, practice filters, daily picks, and settings also stay on this iPhone. The app does not include third-party wordbooks. It reads an import file only when you choose it from Files.

Apple Translation

On iOS 18 or later, viewing a Chrome-synced word without a translation can use Apple's on-device Translation framework to translate the word and its latest saved original context. iOS may ask to download a language pack first. Both translations are saved on this iPhone before any optional Google Drive sync, which includes them in your vocabulary snapshot in the hidden app-data folder. Imported wordbooks are not translated automatically. On iOS 17, missing translations remain pending.

Google Drive sync

After you connect a Google account, the app requests access only to its hidden Google Drive app-data folder, appDataFolder. It downloads, merges, and uploads device snapshots of Chrome-captured vocabulary, including words, translations, source URLs and context, review schedules, and deletion markers. iPhone ratings for Chrome words also sync as separate learning-history files. These contain normalized word text, review time, new-word flag, and rating, so daily counts and weak-word practice can be restored on another iPhone.

Automatic sync is on by default and is attempted at launch, on return to the foreground, and after a rating. You can turn it off in Settings and still use Sync now. iOS does not guarantee background sync while the app is closed. Imported wordbooks and their progress, settings, practice filters, and daily picks are not uploaded to Drive. If sync fails, local words and ratings remain saved for a later retry.

The app reads the authorized account identifier and email address through the Google Drive API to show the sync account and prevent an existing notebook from being uploaded to a different account. Google Sign-In handles authentication. Files are stored per device in your Google account and do not appear in the normal My Drive file list. EchoWords has no developer backend and does not send your notebook to a developer server, advertising service, or analytics provider.

Source links and reminders

When you choose to open a word's source, your browser visits the original website and that site's privacy policy applies. Daily study reminders are scheduled locally by iOS. Notification permission is requested only when you enable reminders. Notifications contain no words, context, or source URLs.

Retention, deletion, and sign-out

You can delete a word synced from Chrome in the app. A deletion marker is saved locally first and propagates to other devices on later sync. Signing out stops Drive access but keeps local data and its original account binding. Removing the iPhone app deletes that device's local data but does not automatically delete Drive files. You control cloud app data through your Google account and can remove it through Google Drive's app management.

Contact

For privacy or data deletion questions, contact acboo2020@gmail.com. We will update this policy when our data practices change.
